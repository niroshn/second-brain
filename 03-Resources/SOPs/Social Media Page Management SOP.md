# Social Media Page Management SOP

Applies to social media page management for Figuro ([[Figuro Marketing]]).

**Purpose:** To ensure all customer comments, enquiries, complaints, and positive feedback are handled professionally, consistently, and quickly across all social media pages.

## 1. General Rules
- Check all social media pages regularly throughout the day.
- Respond politely and professionally to every genuine customer enquiry.
- Never argue with customers publicly.
- Never use rude, defensive, or emotional language.
- Do not delete genuine negative feedback unless it violates the platform's rules or contains spam, harassment, threats, or repeated malicious comments.
- When possible, move detailed customer conversations to DM, email, or WhatsApp.
- Record important customer enquiries and complaints so they can be followed up.

## 2. Customer Comments Containing an Email Address

### Action
If a customer leaves their email address in a comment:
1. Copy the email address.
2. Send them an email with the relevant information.
3. Reply to the social media comment briefly and professionally.
4. If necessary, ask them to check their email.

### Example Comment Reply
> Hi! Thanks for reaching out. We've sent the details to your email. Please check your inbox, and feel free to let us know if you have any questions. 😊

### Important
Do not post detailed pricing, order information, or private customer information publicly.

## 3. Customer Comments Containing a Mobile/WhatsApp Number

### Action
If a customer provides a mobile number:
1. Save/copy the number.
2. Try to contact the customer through WhatsApp.
3. Introduce yourself and explain that you are contacting them regarding their social media enquiry.
4. Provide the requested information.
5. If they do not respond, do not repeatedly message them.

### Example WhatsApp Message
> Hi! This is [Name] from Figuro. You recently shared your WhatsApp number in a comment on our social media page. I'm reaching out to help with your enquiry. Please let me know what information you need, and I'll be happy to assist. 😊

## 4. Comments Saying "SCAM", "FAKE", or Similar

Negative comments must be handled calmly.

### First Occurrence
If someone comments "SCAM", "FAKE", or something similar for the first time, respond politely without arguing.

### Approved Reply
> I think you may be referring to a different company. We are a genuine business, and customer satisfaction is very important to us.
> If you've placed an order with us, please share your order number here or send us a DM, and we'll be happy to look into it.
> For reference, all orders older than 3 weeks have already been delivered. 😊

### Important
- Do not accuse the person of lying.
- Do not insult or attack them.
- Do not get into a long public argument.
- Do not make claims that cannot be verified.
- Encourage genuine customers to provide their order number so the team can investigate.

## 5. Repeated "SCAM" Comments From the Same Person

If the same person repeatedly posts "SCAM", "FAKE", or similar comments after the matter has already been addressed:

### Action
- Do not continue arguing.
- Check whether they are an actual customer.
- If they have an unresolved genuine order issue, escalate it to customer support.
- If they are repeatedly posting the same comments without providing an order number or legitimate information, stop engaging.
- If the behaviour becomes spam, harassment, or repeated malicious posting, block/restrict the account according to the platform's moderation tools.

### Important
Blocking should not be the first response to a genuine complaint. First determine whether the person is an actual customer and whether there is an unresolved issue.

## 6. Positive Feedback From Previous Customers

When an old customer sends a message with positive feedback, always respond warmly and personally.

Do not use a one-word response such as: "Thanks", "Thank you", "Great", "Appreciate it".

Instead, acknowledge their specific feedback.

### Example
> Thank you so much for taking the time to share this with us! It genuinely means a lot to our team to hear that you're happy with your figurine. We put a lot of care into every order, so receiving feedback like this makes all the hard work worthwhile. We truly appreciate your support. ❤️

### Another Example
> This honestly made our day! 😊 Thank you so much for coming back and sharing your experience with us. We're really happy to know that you loved the final result. Your support and kind words mean a lot to our entire team.

## 7. Encourage Customers to Leave a Facebook Review

When an existing customer provides genuine positive feedback, try to turn that feedback into a public review where appropriate.

### Process
1. Thank the customer sincerely.
2. Ask whether they would be comfortable leaving the same feedback as a Facebook review.
3. Send them the Facebook review link if appropriate.
4. Never pressure customers to leave a positive review.
5. Never offer compensation in exchange for a positive review.

### Example Message
> We're really happy to hear that you enjoyed your figurine! ❤️ Your feedback genuinely means a lot to us.
> If you have a moment, we'd really appreciate it if you could share your experience as a review on our Facebook page as well. It helps other customers feel more confident when choosing a custom figurine company.
> Thank you again for supporting our small business! 🙏

## 8. Customers Asking About Pricing

If someone asks about pricing in a comment:
- Provide the correct current starting price if appropriate.
- If pricing depends heavily on size, number of people, complexity, or customization, move the conversation to DM.
- Never guess a price.
- If unsure, ask the sales/customer support team before responding.

### Example
> Hi! 😊 Pricing depends on the size and customization. Please send us a DM with your photo and what you're looking for, and we'll be happy to provide the details.

## 9. Customers Asking About Delivery/Orders

If a customer asks about an existing order:
1. Ask for their order number.
2. Do not request private information publicly.
3. Move the conversation to DM/email when necessary.
4. Check the order status internally.
5. Give the customer a clear update.
6. Escalate delayed or unusual orders to the appropriate team.

### Example
> Hi! We'd be happy to check this for you. Please send us your order number by DM, and our team will look into the status for you. 😊

## 10. Angry or Unhappy Customers

When a customer is unhappy:

### DO
- Stay calm.
- Acknowledge their concern.
- Ask for their order number.
- Move the conversation to DM.
- Investigate the issue.
- Escalate when necessary.

### DON'T
- Argue.
- Blame the customer.
- Mock the customer.
- Delete the comment simply because it is negative.
- Make promises you cannot keep.
- Reveal private customer/order information publicly.

### Example
> We're sorry to hear that you've had this experience. We'd definitely like to look into this for you. Please send us your order number by DM, and we'll check the details and get back to you.

## 11. Daily Social Media Checklist

The social media manager should check:

**Comments**
- New customer enquiries
- Email addresses
- WhatsApp/mobile numbers
- Pricing questions
- Order questions
- Delivery questions
- Positive feedback
- Negative feedback
- "SCAM" / "FAKE" comments
- Spam comments
- Repeated comments from the same person

**Messages**
- Facebook/Instagram DMs
- Customer enquiries
- Existing customer conversations
- Unanswered messages
- Follow-ups requiring action

**Customer Relationship**
- Thank customers for positive feedback
- Identify genuine happy customers
- Ask appropriate customers for a Facebook review
- Record important complaints
- Escalate unresolved issues

## 12. Escalation Rules

Immediately escalate to the manager/team when:
- A customer claims they never received an order.
- A customer claims they received a damaged product.
- A customer requests a refund.
- A customer threatens legal action or a chargeback.
- A customer posts evidence/photos of a serious issue.
- A customer claims fraud or unauthorized payment.
- You are unsure how to respond.
- A viral negative comment starts receiving significant engagement.
- A social media issue could seriously affect the company's reputation.

When in doubt: do not make promises. Escalate first.

## 13. Golden Rule

Every comment represents a public interaction with a potential customer.

The objective is not to win arguments.

The objective is to:

Respond → Understand → Help → Resolve → Build Trust

Always remain professional, friendly, and respectful.
